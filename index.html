About

I am a PhD student in Applied Linguistics and Technology at Iowa State University, researching how learners develop L2 writing and how technology, from corpora to generative AI, can support that development.

My research focuses on corpus linguistics, language assessment, L2 writing development, and AI-generated feedback. I bring a background in teaching English in Vietnam and the United States, and I have designed and taught courses on public speaking and online language teaching.
